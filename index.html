Getting Started
Self Registration

Start by registering yourself on the platform as a participant.

1
Access the hackathon registration portal via the provided link.
2
Click "Register" or "Sign Up" on the homepage.
3
Fill in your personal details — name, email, department, etc.
4
Submit your registration form to complete sign-up.
✅You are now successfully registered as a participant.
2
Define Your Idea
Create & Describe Your Project

Once registered, define what you want to build for the hackathon.

1
Navigate to "Create Project" from your dashboard.
2
Enter your Project Title — make it memorable and descriptive.
3
Write a Brief Description of what problem you're solving.
4
Add your Approach / Idea Summary outlining your solution.
5
Save your project to publish it on the platform.
💡Tip: Keep your description clear and compelling — it helps attract strong teammates.
3
Build Your Team
Invite Team Members

Build your team by inviting other participants to join your project.

1
Go to your project dashboard.
2
Select "Invite Members" from the project menu.
3
Search for participants by name or enter their details directly.
4
Send your invitations to the selected participants.
📌Invited members will receive a notification and can choose to accept or decline.
4
Alternative Path
Join an Existing Team

You don't have to create a project — you can join an existing team instead.

1
Browse the list of available teams and projects on the platform.
2
Select a team whose project aligns with your interests and skills.
3
Click "Request to Join" on the team's project page.
⏳Wait for the team owner to review and approve your request before you are officially added.
5
Team Management
Manage Incoming Requests

As a team creator, you'll receive and manage join requests from other participants.

1
Go to your team dashboard to view all pending requests.
2
Review each request — check the participant's profile and interests.
3
Approve or Decline each request based on your team's needs.
✅Once approved, the participant is officially added as a member of your team.
6
Explore & Network
View All Teams

Explore what others are building across the hackathon.

1
Navigate to the "Teams" or "Projects" section from the main menu.
2
Browse all registered teams — names, project ideas, and members.
3
Use this to network, find collaborators, or get inspired by others' ideas.
💡A great opportunity to spot potential cross-team collaboration or track the competition!
